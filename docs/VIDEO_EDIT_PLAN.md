## Live footage and integrity

Use the November 20 phone portrait and desktop capture as the main pair. Show the desktop in about two-thirds of the canvas and the phone in the remaining area; crop excess ceiling while keeping head movements visible. The earlier wide pair is a different session and must remain labelled as such.

Synchronize before selecting exact cuts. Audio cross-correlation did not provide a trustworthy offset in the inspection, so use an unmistakable shared event, or manually validate gestures against multiple slide changes. Do not assume equal source timestamps are synchronized. Confirm alignment early and late. Cut both angles together between actions; keep real gesture-to-response timing intact.

Use at least 40 seconds of actual simultaneous presenter and desktop demonstration; the plan allocates 75 seconds to lighting tests. Rebalance durations if footage quality requires it. Include a brief lighting-change lead-in for each condition.

## Sound and visual direction

Use the approved dark and amber branding and GoPaddy motion style. Keep captions bold, readable and clear of controls. Narration, captions, cursor focus and clicks should describe the same action. Use one clear narration track for the intro and walkthrough, with quiet music. Keep original room sound from one source in the live section.

Condition captions: “Room light on”; “Room light off · curtain open”; “Room light off · curtain closed”. For the last condition use “Control continued in this test; presenter reported less consistent responses.” Avoid claims about darkness, accuracy, disability-user testing, or blink control that this footage cannot establish.
